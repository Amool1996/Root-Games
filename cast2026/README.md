# Root growth videos (CAST 2026 poster)

3-D OpenSimRoot simulations of long- and short-CCL maize root systems over 40 days.

- **Low nitrogen:** `LowN_long_vs_short_v2.mp4` (soil colour = nitrate)
- **Drought:** `Drought_long_vs_short_v2.mp4` (soil colour = water; rain stops on day 10)

Watch both on the web page: https://amool1996.github.io/Root-Games/cast2026/

Poster: *Longer root cortical cells improve maize growth under suboptimal nitrogen and water in silico* — Amool Singh Vadithya, Jasleen Kaur, Ruairidh Sawers and Jagdeep Singh Sidhu, University of Missouri.
