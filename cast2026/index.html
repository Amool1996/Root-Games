Longer root cortical cells improve maize growth under suboptimal nitrogen and water in silico

Amool Singh Vadithya, Jasleen Kaur, Ruairidh Sawers and Jagdeep Singh Sidhu · CAST 2026 poster

Each video shows 40 days of root growth simulated in OpenSimRoot, in a 150 cm deep soil block. The long-CCL plant is on the left and the short-CCL plant on the right. Press play, or tap the video on a phone.

Soil colour shows nitrate. Long-CCL roots grow deeper and capture nitrate that short-CCL roots leave behind.
