🌽 Root Games

Two games about the half of the plant you never see.

Design & race

Root Race

Spin for a field, build a corn root from six choices, then watch it grow against the champion root. There is no single best root — a clever root matches its field.

PLAY ▶ Three mini games

Amazing Roots!

Steer a growing root through the soil, label a real maize cross-section, then run a farm on a $100 budget and try to turn a profit.
