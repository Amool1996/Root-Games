HOW TO GET A NATURAL, HUMAN-SOUNDING VOICE FOR ROOTY'S STORY
=============================================================

The game narrates itself with a voice built into Windows. On most laptops that
is "Zira", which sounds like a computer. There are three ways up from there,
from easiest to best.


OPTION 1 - FREE, AUTOMATIC, NEEDS INTERNET AT THE BOOTH
--------------------------------------------------------
Start the game with  Start-Root-Race.bat  (it opens Microsoft Edge). If the
laptop is online, Edge offers Microsoft's "Natural" voices and the game picks
one automatically (Aria or Jenny - warm, clear, American English). Nothing to
install, nothing to record. If the internet drops, it quietly falls back to
Zira. Test it once at the venue: press PLAY and listen to the first page.


OPTION 2 - GENERATE MP3s ONLINE ONCE, THEN IT WORKS OFFLINE FOREVER
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Any text-to-speech website that lets you download MP3s will do. Free tiers that
are enough for this (about 15 short clips):

  - Microsoft Azure "Speech Studio" (speech.microsoft.com) - voices "Jenny",
    "Aria", or "Ana" (a child's voice kids like)
  - ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io) - very natural; pick a warm, friendly female voice
  - Google Cloud Text-to-Speech demo, or any "text to speech mp3" site

Steps:
  1. Open README.txt in this folder. It has the exact words for every page.
     If you only want the booth version, use the SHORT wording for the eight
     pages listed under "WHICH ONES MATTER MOST".
  2. Paste one page's text, choose the voice, set the speed a little slower
     than normal (about 0.9x), and download the MP3.
  3. Save it in THIS folder with the page's name: p01.mp3, p08.mp3, ...
  4. Open the game. Any page that has a file uses it; the rest use the
     built-in voice. No settings to change.

Please do not use a celebrity's voice or a cartoon character's voice, even if
a website offers it. That is someone's likeness and is not ours to use at a
university event. A warm "kind teacher" voice is exactly right for this age.


OPTION 3 - A REAL PERSON (BEST FOR SMALL CHILDREN)
---------------------------------------------------
Someone in the lab reads the eight short pages into a phone. See README.txt
("HOW TO RECORD"). Ten minutes, and it beats every synthetic voice.
