ROOTY'S STORY — optional recorded voice-over
============================================

The story already narrates itself using a female voice built into the computer
(on this laptop that is "Microsoft Zira"). It works with no internet and needs
no setup. But a built-in voice still sounds like a computer.

If you want it to sound like a real teacher, record the 13 pages as MP3 files
and drop them in THIS folder. The game finds them automatically — no code
changes, no settings. If a file is missing, that page falls back to the
built-in voice, so you can even do a few pages at a time.

FILE NAMES (exactly these, lower case):

  p01.mp3   Hello, young scientists! My name is Rooty, and I am a root.
            I live under the ground, where nobody can see me.

  p02.mp3   Look at this corn plant. On top, you can see the green leaves.
            But almost half of the plant is hidden under the soil.
            That hidden half, is me.

  p03.mp3   My first job is to hold on tight. When the wind blows hard,
            I grip the soil with all my might. That is why the plant does
            not fall over.

  p04.mp3   My second job is to drink. I pull water out of the soil, all day
            long. On a hot day, one corn plant can drink a big bottle of water.

  p05.mp3   My third job is to eat. Plants need food from the soil, just like
            you need food on your plate. Nitrogen makes the leaves big and
            green. Phosphorus helps the roots grow strong.

  p06.mp3   Now, let me show you my secret. Look very, very close. I am covered
            in thousands of tiny hairs. They are too small for your eyes to
            see, but they do most of the drinking. More hairs, means more
            water and more food.

  p07.mp3   How do I grow? Only my very tip grows. It pushes between the bits
            of soil, a little bit further every day. In one month, I can grow
            deeper than you are tall.

  p08.mp3   Everything I drink, and everything I eat, travels up. Up, up, up to
            the leaves. And that is how the plant grows big and strong.

  p09.mp3   But here is the tricky part. Some summers, it hardly rains at all.
            Can you guess where the water is hiding? Deep down! So in a dry
            year, the deep root wins.

  p10.mp3   Other years, the soil runs out of nitrogen. Nitrogen is slippery.
            Every time it rains, it washes down, deeper and deeper. So once
            again, the deep root wins.

  p11.mp3   But phosphorus is different. Phosphorus sticks to the soil, right
            near the top. Digging deep will not help you here. This time, the
            short fuzzy root, with all those tiny hairs, wins.

  p12.mp3   So, young scientists, what did we learn? There is no single best
            root. The best root is the one that matches the weather. And that
            is exactly what real scientists study.

  p13.mp3   Now it is your turn. Spin the weather wheel, and build me your way.
            Let us grow the biggest plant in the whole field!

HOW TO RECORD
-------------
Any phone voice recorder works. Read slowly and warmly, the way you would read
to a class of six-year-olds. Pause a beat between sentences. Record each page as
its own file, then copy them here with the names above.

If the recordings come out as .m4a (iPhone) or .wav, convert them to .mp3 first
(any free online converter, or VLC: Media > Convert/Save).

The page turns when the voice stops talking, so the pictures follow your
recording automatically however long it is.
